I collect Pokémon cards. I go to the show every month.

The next one is Westgate, September 5–6 2026. Saturday and Sunday.

This is not nostalgia I trot out at dinner. It is a practice. I look at print runs. I know what a bad sleeve does to a card. I have a box, and I keep it in order.

The show is a room of people who also take it seriously. Some of them are kids. Some of them have been doing this since the nineties. I like being in a hobby that still happens on tables.

I buy cards I want to hold. I do not treat it like a portfolio. If a card goes up, fine. If it does not, I still have the card.
