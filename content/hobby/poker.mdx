I play poker. The SF Social Club is part of that scene. A lot of things happen there. I will not list them.

I play to play. I am not building a brand off it. I am not a grindset account. I like the cards, the people, the hour when the table gets honest.

A hobby should not need a thesis. This one has a little: you find out how you act when it costs something. I like that information.

Then I go home.
