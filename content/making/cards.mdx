Pokémon cards. I go to the card show every month. This is a current practice, not
a story about being nine years old.

What I like is that it is a real market run by real people. You learn to grade,
to price, to spot a fake, to walk away. You shake hands and remember faces. It is
a small economy that rewards paying attention.

The cards are the excuse. The practice is showing up.
