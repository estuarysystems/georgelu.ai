Poker is a hobby and a social one. Most of it is the SF Social Club — the room,
the regulars, the night itself.

I like the game because it is honest. The cards do not care how you feel and
neither does the math. You make decisions with incomplete information and then
you find out. Over enough hands, who you are shows up.

Play, not hustle. The point is the table and the people at it.
