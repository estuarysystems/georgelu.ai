As a kid I wanted to run a server and never did — no hardware, no idea how. So I
am building it now.

It is a good excuse to keep my hands on the real machinery: a box, a network, the
uptime, the mods, the small community that shows up. The nine-year-old wanted a
world he controlled. Turns out that is just systems work with better stakes.

Running the thing you once only got to play in is its own kind of finished
business.
