Most people pick a side. The business people wait on the engineers. The engineers wait on a spec. I take on problems I can see, then I build the company or the tool.

I live in the Bay Area. I run Estuary Systems, an AI agency. We make new AI products for people who already have a business. I also build a claims system for people law firms will not represent.

I care about how people dress and carry themselves. I think you should build in the real world instead of collecting credentials. I think a family is a life, not a lifestyle brand. Privacy and security matter more now, because the new products sit inside other people's work.

This site is a hobby-resume. Drive it like a console.
