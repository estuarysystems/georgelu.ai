A lot of good claims never get taken. Too small. Too slow. Too annoying for the institution’s economics.

I am building software so those claims can still be litigated.

The small person gets dropped when the institution’s math does not match their case. Software can change that math.

I am not romantic about courts. I am romantic about not letting a clean claim die for billable-hour reasons.

Estuary is work with companies. This is work for people who do not have one standing behind them.
