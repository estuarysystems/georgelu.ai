There is a whole class of claims that are real, winnable, and too small for a
law firm to bother with. The math does not work for them, so the person with the
claim just eats the loss. That happens millions of times a year.

I am building a system to litigate those claims. Software carries the parts that
made them uneconomical — the intake, the paperwork, the tracking — so a valid
claim can actually be pursued instead of quietly dropped.

This is the clearest piece of why I build. See a person getting steamrolled by
the fact that nobody will represent them, then build the tool that lets them
fight back.
