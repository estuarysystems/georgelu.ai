Law firms will not take a lot of good claims. The case is real. The person is right. The dollar amount is too small, or too slow, or too annoying. So the firm passes, and the person eats it.

I am building a system to litigate those claims anyway.

This is the clearest reason I build anything. The small guy gets dropped because the institution's economics are not his economics. Software can change the math. Paperwork, deadlines, evidence, the boring spine of a case — that is work a system can carry so a person does not need a firm's overhead to be heard.

I am not romantic about courts. I am romantic about not letting a clean claim die because nobody's billable hour wanted it.

If Estuary is how I work with companies, this is how I want to work for people who do not have one.
