Estuary Systems is the agency. I run it.

We build new AI products for companies that already do something real. Not pitch decks. Products. The work sits on the seam I like: what the business actually needs, and what the model can be made to do without lying.

I care about security and privacy more than I care about the demo. If your product reads a customer's world, you do not get to be casual about where that world goes. Most AI work I see is a wrapper with a smile. We do not ship that.

A typical job looks like this. Someone has a messy process and a pile of documents, or a team drowning in work a model could draft. I sit with them until I can see the job. Then we build the smallest thing that does the job, and we put real controls around the data.

That is the work. 2026.
