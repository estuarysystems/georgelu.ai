Using AI well is not a prompt trick. It is business sense.

Most companies do not need “an AI project.” They need to know what is actually broken, which bottleneck is worth a tool, and where money should go first. Without that, you buy demos and call it transformation.

Estuary is Done-With-You.

Three layers:

1. Teach you how to build AI apps — so your team is not helpless when the vendor leaves the room.
2. Bring business sense to what you build — bottlenecks, sequencing, where spend lands, what to kill.
3. Still build it with you when you want hands on the keyboard — standard, not the whole product.

The pitch is not “I will make AI for you.”

The pitch is: can you afford to stay behind on knowing how this works — and not have someone in your corner on what changed this month?

I want a chance to demonstrate that sense on your business. What could improve. Where you are stuck. Where money is best spent.

If that thinking is useful, we keep working together. If it is not, you still leave clearer than you arrived.
