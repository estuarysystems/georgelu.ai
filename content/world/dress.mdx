name: Dress
title: Look like you meant to leave the house.
blurb: Bringing back the social norm of dressing on purpose.
order: 1
---

There is an America in the good books and the good movies where people wear
clothes on purpose. They dressed for each other. Leaving the house was a small
act of respect for everyone who would see you.

We traded that for comfort and called it freedom. It is mostly just lower
standards. Dressing well is not about money or labels. It is about deciding that
the people around you are worth the five minutes.

Bring back the norm. Look like you meant to leave the house.
