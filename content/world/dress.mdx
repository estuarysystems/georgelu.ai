People embody the success they want before they take action to achieve it. Dressing is one way they do that.

I keep a story in my head. Seven guys in suits. One guy without. In that room, the person not wearing the suit is the most important person.

That idea trickled far into people's heads. They embody "successful software engineer" as someone who dresses very casually. Even poorly. Because that engineer is so important that nobody will enforce dress standards. They do not want to risk the engineer being uncomfortable and leaving.

That is why tech dresses casually. I think that is a bad thing.

Dress to show you care about your appearance. Dress to show you want to be pleasant to be around.
