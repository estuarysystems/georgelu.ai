People often embody the success they want before they earn it. Dress is one way.

Seven people in suits. One without. In that room, the person not in a suit is usually the most important person in it.

That idea leaked into tech: the “successful engineer” costume became casual or poor dress, because that person was too important for anyone to enforce a standard.

I think that is a bad equilibrium.

Dress like you care how you show up. Dress like you intend to be pleasant to be around.
