blurb: An argument about care and a life, not a culture-war post.
order: 2
---

A household is real work. Someone holding it — the meals, the calendar, the
thousand small repairs of a shared life — is not a person who gave something up.
It is a person doing one of the hardest and most important jobs there is.

I want that in my life. Not as a rule for anyone else, and not as a fight. As a
plan for a home where care is the point, not the thing that gets squeezed in
after two careers.

This is an argument about what a good life looks like up close. I think care,
done on purpose by someone who chose it, is worth building around.
