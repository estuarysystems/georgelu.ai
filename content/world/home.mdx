I want a home that is kept. Not staged. Kept.

A stay-at-home spouse is not a luxury brand and it is not a political costume. It is a person who makes a life in a place, on purpose, every day. Someone is there when a kid walks in. Someone knows what is in the fridge. Someone is not performing a commute and calling the leftovers a family.

I am not saying every household has to look like this. I am saying this is the household I am building toward, and I will not apologize for it.

Care is work. It is the work that makes every other kind of work possible. I have watched homes where both people are exhausted and the house is a pit stop. I do not want that.

If you want a life, someone has to tend it. I would rather build a company that can support that than pretend a dual-career grind is the same thing as a family.
