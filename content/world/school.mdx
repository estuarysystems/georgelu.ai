College is a bad place to send a kid and a bad way to spend years.

You pay a lot of money to sit in rooms with people who have never shipped anything. You learn to write for a professor. You collect a credential that used to mean you could do a job. Now it means you waited.

I want kids who can build and sell. Who have been in a real room with a real customer. Who know what a payroll feels like, or a product that breaks on a Friday.

School is fine for some trades. It is a holding pen for most of the rest. The better path is work: an apprenticeship, a business, a tool you made that someone paid for.

I will not send a kid into debt to find themselves. They can find themselves on a job site or in a shop. The world is still there. It does not require a campus.
