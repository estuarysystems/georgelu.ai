title: College is a bad place to send a kid.
blurb: Building and doing real business is the better path.
order: 3
---

Four years and a large bill to be told what to read and when. For most people it
is a bad place to send a kid and a bad way to spend years.

The alternative is not "do nothing." It is build something. Start the small
business, ship the tool, take the job where you learn by being useful. You find
out fast what you are good at and what you actually like. The feedback is real
because money and other people are involved.

Credentials are a proxy. Building is the thing itself. Given the choice, do the
thing.
