College is a bad default place to send a kid and a bad default way to spend years.

You pay to sit with people who never shipped. The credential often means you waited.

I want kids who can build and sell. School is fine for some trades. For most people it is a holding pen.

Better path: apprenticeship, a business, a tool someone will pay for.

I will not send a kid into debt to “find themselves.”
